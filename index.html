DECENTRALIZE DESIGN

THE INTERNET
IS STILL FLAT.

We are spatial beings living in a two-dimensional digital world. Our screens are windows, but what lies beyond is still mostly flat—pages, feeds, grids. We believe digital space can be something more. Not a simulation of the physical world, but an entirely new realm with its own language, logic, and possibilities.

01 / THE PROBLEM

WE TRAPPED INFINITY
IN A RECTANGLE.

Think about how you experience the digital world: scrolling through rectangles on a rectangle, tapping icons arranged in grids. We've created a boundless virtual realm, yet we're still designing it like paper. We have the freedom to build spaces that defy gravity, transcend time zones, and exist in impossible geometries—but instead, we keep making digital versions of physical things.

02 / THE INSIGHT

DIGITAL SPACE
SPEAKS ITS OWN
LANGUAGE.

Imagine designing a building without gravity. Without walls that need to support ceilings. Without materials that decay. This isn't science fiction—it's the everyday reality of digital space. Yet most virtual environments still pretend to follow physical rules. A virtual gallery with walls. A digital office with cubicles. A metaverse cafe with chairs bolted to the floor.

What if we stopped pretending? What if we let digital space be what it actually is?

"A digital chair doesn't need to support weight—it only needs to suggest the idea of support. The function becomes purely symbolic, at least for now."

03 / OUR PHILOSOPHY

RELIGIOUS
ROBOTICS.

This is what we call our approach: Religious Robotics. Not making things look cool or futuristic, but letting digital space exist on its own terms. No gravity unless we want it. No solid masses unless they serve a purpose. Chrome surfaces, floating forms, impossible structures—these aren't just aesthetic choices. They're acts of honesty.

Think of it like teaching a new language. You can't just translate word-for-word from English to Japanese and expect it to make sense. Each language has its own grammar, its own logic. Digital space is the same. It needs its own vocabulary, its own rules—or rather, its own freedom from rules.

Like Lebbeus Woods' drawings that were never meant to be built—they were attempts to free architecture from its own limitations. That's what we're doing in code instead of paper. Letting digital space be what it actually is: minimal, spiritual, technically impossible in real life. When your chair doesn't need to support weight, suddenly you can focus on making it support the soul.

04 / THE VISION

SPACES THAT
THINK, BREATHE,
REMEMBER.

Imagine a museum that remembers your footsteps.

As you walk through, it learns which artworks captivate you, which corners invite contemplation. The next visitor experiences the space differently, shaped by the collective memory of everyone who came before. Like a city that grows wiser with every footstep.

Imagine a theater where you choose your perspective.

Not just from a fixed seat, but from inside the scene, floating above the stage, or walking alongside the performers. The story unfolds differently for each viewer, yet everyone shares the same moment. Time becomes fluid—replay a dancer's leap from inside the arc of motion.

Imagine a retail space that tells stories.

Where every product sits within a narrative environment. A fashion collection displayed in a crystalline cavern. Digital artifacts arranged in an impossible forest. Shopping becomes exploration, commerce becomes culture, browsing becomes discovery.

Imagine events that transcend time zones.

A fashion show at midnight in New York, noon in Tokyo, yet somehow the perfect hour for everyone. Thousands attend, each from their own perfect viewpoint. The collective gasp when something beautiful happens—deeply personal yet undeniably shared.

05 / THE MEDIUM

VIRTUAL THEATER:
A NEW MEDIUM.

We pioneered something we call Virtual Theater. It's not cinema—there's no fixed camera, no edited sequence. It's not traditional theater—there's no physical stage, no limitation of seats. It's something entirely new: a performance that happens in real-time, in virtual space, where you can watch from designated angles, wander freely through the scene, or even become an actor yourself.

Think of it as the synthesis of cinema's visual language and theater's liveness, but freed from the constraints of both. The performance unfolds around you. Time can bend. Perspectives can shift. Reality becomes fluid.

CINEMATIC MODE

Watch from designated camera angles. Like Hitchcock directing your gaze, but you choose when to cut.

IMMERSIVE MODE

Wander through the performance like a ghost. Experience moments from impossible angles.

ACTOR MODE

Step into the story. Walk Hamlet's castle and become the prince yourself.

06 / ACCESSIBILITY

NO DOWNLOADS.
NO BARRIERS.
JUST CLICK.

Everything we create lives in your browser. No apps to download, no accounts to create, no expensive hardware required. Just a link, a click, and you're inside a three-dimensional world. This isn't just convenient—it's philosophical.

The web was meant to be open, accessible, instant. We're bringing that ethos to spatial design. Whether you're on a high-end gaming PC or a basic smartphone, whether you're in Milan or Mumbai, whether you're 8 or 80—you can experience these spaces.

07D / METHODOLOGY

HOW WE
BUILD.

Technical Foundation

Every space begins with performance. We optimize mesh geometry, implement LOD strategies, and use efficient texture mapping. glTF/GLB format for universal compatibility. PBR materials for realistic shading without heavy computation.

Smart objects coordinate everything from lighting changes to character movements. Chain reactions trigger seamlessly. NPCs guide visitors. Environments adapt in real-time.

Design Philosophy

Sound comes first—we build spaces around ambient sets, letting audio guide spatial rhythm. Pacing learned from TikTok: constant stimulation punctuated by silence. Lighting as narrative: spaces that respond to presence, that remember your path.

Platforms: Hyperfy, Mona, Oncyber. Each chosen for specific strengths. All browser-based. All accessible. All pushing boundaries of what's possible without downloads.

08 / PRINCIPLES

THREE CORE
PRINCIPLES.

1. Spatial Honesty

We don't fake physics. We don't pretend digital space needs walls or floors unless they serve a purpose. Every element exists because it enhances the experience, not because "that's how buildings work." This honesty creates spaces that feel authentic to their medium.

2. Technical Excellence

Beauty means nothing if it doesn't load. Every space we create is optimized to run smoothly in browsers—under 15MB, instant access, 60fps. We obsess over file sizes, rendering efficiency, and performance because accessibility is not optional.

3. Narrative Architecture

Every space tells a story. Not through text or labels, but through form, light, movement, and interaction. A spiral that climbs suggests growth. A cave invites intimacy. A floating platform promises freedom. The architecture itself is the narrative.

09 / THE FUTURE

COLLECTIVE
IMAGINATION.

We're not building the metaverse. We're not creating the future of the internet. Those phrases are too grand, too final. What we're doing is simpler and more profound: we're exploring. Every project is an experiment. Every space is a question.

What happens when a museum can reshape itself based on who's visiting? How does fashion change when runways can exist in impossible geometries? What new forms of theater emerge when viewers can float through scenes? What does ownership mean when spaces can be forked, remixed, and evolved collectively?

We don't have all the answers. We're discovering them together, one space at a time.
