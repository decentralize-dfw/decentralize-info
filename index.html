DIGITAL PRISON
INFINITY TRAPPED IN A RECTANGLE
BOUNDLESS REALM, PAPER DESIGN
DIGITAL MUST FIND ITS OWN VOICE
We've created a boundless virtual realm, yet we're still designing it like paper. Websites are documents. Apps are pages. Even our immersive experiences are just rectangles we strap to our faces. Film learned montage. Television learned the close-up. Digital space is still mimicking the past.

02 / OUR PHILOSOPHY
DIGITAL AUTONOMY
RELIGIOUS ROBOTICS
LET DIGITAL BE ITSELF
NO GRAVITY, NO LIMITS, NO RULES
Physical space has rules. Gravity pulls you down. Materials cost money. Time moves forward. But digital space has none of these constraints. We can float. We can build without materials. We can freeze time or loop it infinitely. Religious Robotics means embracing what makes digital fundamentally different.
